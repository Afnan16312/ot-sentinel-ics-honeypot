# Ethics, safety and publication policy

This project is a passive, low-interaction research sensor. It is not connected to real industrial equipment, does not represent a production control system, and must never be deployed inside an operational network.

## Commitments

- No retaliation, counter-scanning, credential testing or interaction with source systems.
- No execution of uploaded programs, shell commands or malware.
- No deliberate collection beyond the minimum protocol telemetry required for analysis.
- No public release of raw source IP addresses, exact payloads, credentials or personal data.
- No attribution of activity to a person, organization or country from IP information alone.
- No claims that the observations represent all UAE threats or demonstrate NESA/UAE regulatory compliance.
- Immediate shutdown if the sensor causes abuse reports, unexpected outbound traffic or resource instability.

## Accurate reporting language

Use: “Activity observed by an Internet-exposed ICS decoy hosted in Azure UAE North during the stated collection window.”

Do not use: “UAE attackers,” “attacks against UAE critical infrastructure,” or “confirmed exploitation” unless independent evidence actually supports those statements.

## Responsible disclosure

If telemetry appears to contain a previously unknown vulnerability or sensitive third-party information, do not publish it. Preserve a minimal private record, contact the affected vendor or appropriate CERT through established channels, and allow coordinated remediation.
